Offline Translator

Quick Introduction

A speech-to-speech translator running on a Raspberry Pi that works without an internet connection. Just funded by Nord Anglia's Social Impact Grant for further development. Look at it in action here!

Preface

This project started almost a year ago, right before a trip to Japan. I really wanted to speak Japanese and communicate with the local people, but with the trip looming ahead there was no time to learn Japanese. So, I put my engineering skills to good use and started developing an offline translator.
